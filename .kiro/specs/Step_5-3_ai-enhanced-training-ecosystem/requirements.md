# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This feature implements an AI-Enhanced Training Ecosystem (Phase 5.3) that transforms the existing training management system (3.3) from a static content delivery model into an intelligent, adaptive learning platform. The ecosystem comprises five interconnected capabilities: an AI Training Planner that maps document requirements to user roles and identifies skill gaps; an AI Training Material Generator that produces presentations, summaries, and educational content from SOPs and Work Instructions; an Automated Question Generator that creates comprehension quizzes and assessments directly from documents; Role-Play Scenarios that simulate "Virtual Audits" where employees must demonstrate document comprehension in conversational interactions; and Dynamic Feedback that guides users to exact document paragraphs when they answer incorrectly.

The system builds upon the existing training content generator (placeholder DSPy pipeline), the Educational Specialist agent archetype (5.1), the vLLM inference layer (4.3), and the RAG knowledge base (4.2). Generated assessments integrate with the training-gated access control (3.5) to enforce compliance before document access is granted.

## Glossary

- **Training_Planner**: The AI service that analyzes document requirements, user roles, and existing training records to generate personalized training schedules and identify skill gaps per employee.
- **Training_Material_Generator**: The AI service that produces structured educational content (presentations, summaries, key takeaways, procedural guides) from source documents using the Educational Specialist agent archetype.
- **Question_Generator**: The AI service that creates comprehension quiz questions, multiple-choice assessments, and scenario-based evaluations derived directly from document content. Extends the existing placeholder quiz generation in `training_content_generator.py`.
- **Role_Play_Engine**: The conversational AI service that conducts interactive "Virtual Audit" sessions where an employee must demonstrate document comprehension by answering contextual questions in a dialogue format.
- **Dynamic_Feedback_Service**: The AI service that, upon an incorrect answer, retrieves and presents the exact paragraph from the source document where the correct information resides, using the RAG embedding system.
- **Training_Schedule**: A persisted plan containing ordered training items with deadlines, priorities, and dependencies for a specific user, generated by the Training_Planner.
- **Skill_Gap**: An identified deficiency where a user's current training records do not satisfy the requirements of documents assigned to their role.
- **Training_Material**: A generated educational artifact (summary, presentation outline, key points, procedural walkthrough) derived from a source document.
- **Assessment**: A set of generated questions (multiple-choice, scenario-based, or role-play) used to verify comprehension of a specific document version.
- **Virtual_Audit_Session**: A single interactive role-play conversation between the Role_Play_Engine and an employee, scored on comprehension accuracy and document reference quality.
- **Paragraph_Reference**: A specific location within a source document (section, paragraph index, text excerpt) identified by the Dynamic_Feedback_Service as containing the answer to a failed question.
- **Educational_Specialist**: The existing agent archetype (from 5.1) with agent_type "generation" used for training content creation, configured with temperature 0.6, max_tokens 8192, and evaluation rubric focused on learning objectives, content effectiveness, assessment quality, and engagement.
- **InferenceClient**: The existing async HTTP client for vLLM communication (from 4.3).
- **KnowledgeService**: The existing RAG service for document retrieval and semantic search (from 4.2).
- **ContentStatus**: The existing enum (pending_review, approved, rejected, draft) governing training content lifecycle.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: AI Training Planner — Schedule Generation

**User Story:** As a training coordinator, I want the system to automatically generate personalized training schedules for employees based on their role, assigned documents, and compliance deadlines, so that I do not have to manually plan training paths for each user.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a training schedule generation is requested for a user, THE Training_Planner SHALL analyze the user's role, assigned document set, existing training records, and document compliance deadlines to produce a Training_Schedule containing ordered training items with priorities and suggested deadlines.
2. THE Training_Planner SHALL use the Educational_Specialist agent archetype (from 5.1) via the InferenceClient to determine training item ordering based on document dependencies, prerequisite knowledge, and regulatory priority.
3. THE Training_Planner SHALL assign priority levels to each training item based on document compliance deadline proximity as follows: Critical when the deadline is within 7 days or already overdue, High when the deadline is within 30 days, Medium when the deadline is within 90 days, and Low when the deadline is more than 90 days away. Documents gated by training-gated access control (3.5) SHALL be elevated by one priority level (e.g., Medium becomes High) regardless of deadline proximity.
4. THE Training_Planner SHALL identify Skill_Gaps by comparing the user's completed training records against all documents assigned to the user's role that require mandatory training, producing a list of documents where training is missing or expired.
5. WHEN a new document version is published that requires training, THE Training_Planner SHALL automatically flag affected users and add the document to their pending training schedules with priority determined by the deadline proximity rules defined in criterion 3, treating the document's compliance deadline as the reference date.
6. THE Training_Planner SHALL expose a POST /api/training/planner/generate endpoint that accepts {user_id, company_id} and returns HTTP 202 with a job_id for the async schedule generation task. This endpoint SHALL require the X-Change-Reason header.
7. THE Training_Planner SHALL expose a GET /api/training/planner/schedule/{user_id} endpoint that returns the current training schedule for a user, including completed items, pending items, skill gaps, and overall compliance percentage computed as (completed required training items for the user / total required training items for the user) × 100, rounded to one decimal place.
8. THE Training_Planner SHALL scope all operations to the company identified by the X-Company-Id header.
9. IF the InferenceClient is unavailable or returns an error during schedule generation, THEN THE Training_Planner SHALL mark the generation job as failed with a reason indicating the inference service was unreachable, and SHALL retain any previously generated schedule for the user unchanged.
10. IF the specified user_id does not exist or has no documents assigned to their role, THEN THE Training_Planner SHALL mark the generation job as failed with a reason indicating no training items could be identified for the user.

### Requirement 2: AI Training Planner — Skill Gap Analysis

**User Story:** As a quality manager, I want a company-wide view of skill gaps across all employees, so that I can identify compliance risks and prioritize training resource allocation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Training_Planner SHALL expose a GET /api/training/planner/gaps endpoint that returns an aggregated skill gap report for the current company, listing: total users with gaps, the top 10 documents with the most untrained users, the top 10 users with the most outstanding training items, and overall company training compliance percentage. The endpoint SHALL support query parameters limit (default 20, maximum 100) and offset (default 0) for paginating the top-documents and top-users lists.
2. THE Training_Planner SHALL compute the company training compliance percentage as: (total completed required training items / total required training items across all users) × 100, rounded to two decimal places.
3. IF the source documents in the gap report have associated regulatory tags, THEN THE Training_Planner SHALL group skill gaps by regulatory framework (e.g., GMP, ISO 13485) and include per-framework compliance percentages in the response.
4. THE Training_Planner SHALL provide a GET /api/training/planner/gaps/{user_id} endpoint that returns the individual skill gap detail for a specific user, including: documents requiring training, days overdue (integer, 0 if not yet past deadline), priority level (Critical, High, Medium, Low), and whether the gap blocks document access via training-gated control.
5. WHEN a training record is created, a training record is invalidated, or a new document version requiring training is published, THE Training_Planner SHALL recalculate skill gaps for all affected users within the same company within 60 seconds of the triggering event.
6. THE Training_Planner SHALL scope all gap analysis to the company identified by the X-Company-Id header.
7. IF the X-Company-Id header is missing or references a non-existent company, THEN THE Training_Planner SHALL return HTTP 400 or HTTP 404 respectively with an error message indicating the issue.
8. IF the user_id in GET /api/training/planner/gaps/{user_id} does not exist or does not belong to the requesting company, THEN THE Training_Planner SHALL return HTTP 404 with an error message indicating the user was not found.

### Requirement 3: AI Training Material Generator

**User Story:** As a training coordinator, I want the system to automatically generate training presentations, summaries, and educational content from uploaded SOPs and Work Instructions, so that I do not have to manually create training materials for every document.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN training material generation is requested for a document, THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL retrieve the document content from MinIO storage and use the Educational_Specialist agent archetype via the InferenceClient to produce structured training materials.
2. THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL generate the following material types from a single document: executive_summary (concise overview for quick reference), detailed_walkthrough (step-by-step procedural guide with explanations), key_takeaways (bullet-point list of critical information), presentation_outline (structured slide-by-slide outline with speaker notes), and safety_highlights (extracted safety-critical information with emphasis markers).
3. THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL structure all generated materials with: learning_objectives (measurable outcomes using Bloom's taxonomy verbs), target_audience (role-based audience identification), estimated_duration (time estimate for material consumption), and prerequisite_documents (list of documents that should be trained on first).
4. THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL produce materials in a structured JSON format that the frontend can render into different presentation views (slide deck, document reader, summary card).
5. WHEN generating materials for a new document version, THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL highlight changes from the previous version by comparing content and producing a "changes_summary" section identifying what is new, modified, or removed.
6. THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL submit all generated materials with ContentStatus "pending_review" requiring coordinator approval before presentation to trainees.
7. THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL expose a POST /api/training/materials/generate endpoint that accepts {document_id, document_version_id, material_types (optional array)} and returns HTTP 202 with a job_id for the async generation task.
8. THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL expose a GET /api/training/materials/{document_id} endpoint that returns all generated materials for a document, filterable by material_type and status, with pagination via limit (default 20, maximum 100) and offset (default 0) query parameters.
9. THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL record generation metadata including: inference_duration_ms, model_used, token_count, and generation_parameters for quality monitoring.
10. THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL scope all operations to the company identified by the X-Company-Id header, and the POST endpoint SHALL require the X-Change-Reason header.
11. IF the document content cannot be retrieved from MinIO storage (file not found, access denied, or storage unavailable), THEN THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL mark the generation job as failed with a reason indicating the document content was inaccessible.
12. IF the document content exceeds the InferenceClient context window, THEN THE Training_Material_Generator SHALL chunk the document into sections and generate materials per-section, then merge the results into a unified material structure.

### Requirement 4: Automated Question Generator

**User Story:** As a training coordinator, I want the system to automatically generate comprehension quizzes and assessments from documents that require mandatory training, so that assessment quality is consistent and creation effort is eliminated.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN question generation is requested for a document, THE Question_Generator SHALL retrieve the document content and use the Educational_Specialist agent archetype via the InferenceClient to produce a set of comprehension questions with validated answers.
2. THE Question_Generator SHALL generate questions in the following formats: multiple_choice (4 options with exactly 1 correct answer), true_false (statement with correct boolean), scenario_based (situational question requiring application of document knowledge with a maximum response length of 500 characters), and fill_in_blank (statement with a domain-specific term or phrase removed, where the removed term appears in the source document section referenced by sop_section_ref).
3. THE Question_Generator SHALL produce for each question: question_text (maximum 1000 characters), question_type, correct_answer (maximum 500 characters), distractors (for multiple_choice), explanation (maximum 500 characters describing why the correct answer is correct), difficulty_level (basic, intermediate, advanced), sop_section_ref (exact section/paragraph reference in the source document), and bloom_taxonomy_level (remember, understand, apply, analyze).
4. THE Question_Generator SHALL generate a minimum of 5 and a maximum of 20 questions per document, with distribution across difficulty levels: at least 30% basic, at least 30% intermediate, and at most 30% advanced, with remaining percentage allocated to basic or intermediate at the system's discretion.
5. THE Question_Generator SHALL validate generated questions by verifying: each correct_answer is factually supported by the referenced sop_section_ref, each distractor is plausible but contradicted by document content, and no two questions reference the same sop_section_ref with the same question_type.
6. THE Question_Generator SHALL produce an answer_key document in structured JSON format that maps each question_id to its question_text, correct_answer, sop_section_ref, and the verbatim source paragraph text for coordinator review.
7. THE Question_Generator SHALL submit all generated questions with ContentStatus "pending_review" requiring coordinator approval before use in assessments.
8. THE Question_Generator SHALL expose a POST /api/training/questions/generate endpoint that accepts {document_id, document_version_id, question_count (optional, default 10, minimum 5, maximum 20), difficulty_distribution (optional)} and returns HTTP 202 with a job_id.
9. THE Question_Generator SHALL expose a GET /api/training/questions/{document_id} endpoint that returns generated questions for a document, filterable by document_version_id, status, difficulty_level, and question_type, with pagination via limit (default 20, maximum 100) and offset (default 0) query parameters.
10. WHEN questions with ContentStatus "approved" exist for a document version, THE Question_Generator SHALL make those questions available to the existing quiz submission system (POST /api/training/quiz/submit) by associating them with the corresponding training content record (content_id) so that the existing quiz-taking flow and training gate (3.5) can use them without modification.
11. THE Question_Generator SHALL scope all operations to the company identified by the X-Company-Id header, and the POST endpoint SHALL require the X-Change-Reason header.
12. IF the document content is insufficient to generate the requested number of questions (fewer than 5 producible), THEN THE Question_Generator SHALL fail the generation job with a status of "failed" and a reason indicating the document lacks sufficient content for the minimum question count.
13. IF question generation is requested for a document_version_id that already has generated questions, THEN THE Question_Generator SHALL create a new generation batch without deleting existing questions, allowing the coordinator to review and approve questions from either batch.
14. IF the InferenceClient fails to respond within 120 seconds or returns an error during generation, THEN THE Question_Generator SHALL mark the generation job as "failed" with a reason indicating the inference service was unavailable, and the coordinator SHALL be able to retry by submitting a new generation request.

### Requirement 5: Automated Grading and Validation

**User Story:** As a system, I want to automatically grade quiz submissions and validate answer correctness using both exact matching and semantic similarity, so that minor phrasing differences in fill-in-blank and scenario-based answers do not unfairly penalize users.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a quiz submission is received for multiple_choice or true_false questions, THE Question_Generator SHALL grade using exact string matching against the correct_answer (maintaining existing behavior from quiz_service.py), and SHALL record a confidence_score of 1.0 for correct answers and 0.0 for incorrect answers.
2. WHEN a quiz submission is received for fill_in_blank questions, THE Question_Generator SHALL grade by computing semantic similarity between the user's response and the correct_answer via the embedding model, applying case-insensitive comparison prior to embedding to normalize input.
3. WHEN a quiz submission is received for scenario_based questions, THE Question_Generator SHALL use the InferenceClient to evaluate the response against the correct_answer and source paragraph, producing a score (0.0–1.0) and explanation of the evaluation. A scenario_based answer SHALL be marked correct if the LLM evaluation score is >= 0.70.
4. THE Question_Generator SHALL apply a semantic similarity threshold of 0.85 for fill_in_blank grading: responses with similarity >= 0.85 to the correct_answer are marked correct.
5. THE Question_Generator SHALL maintain the existing 80% passing threshold (ceil(total_questions × 0.8) correct answers required) for overall quiz pass/fail determination.
6. IF a submitted answer is empty, null, or missing for a question, THEN THE Question_Generator SHALL mark that answer as incorrect with a confidence_score of 0.0 and grading_method "exact" without invoking the embedding model or InferenceClient.
7. THE Question_Generator SHALL record grading metadata for each answer: grading_method (exact, semantic, llm_evaluated), confidence_score (semantic similarity value for fill_in_blank, LLM evaluation score for scenario_based, or 1.0/0.0 for exact match), and time_to_grade_ms.
8. IF the embedding model or InferenceClient is unavailable or fails to respond within 30 seconds during grading of a fill_in_blank or scenario_based answer, THEN THE Question_Generator SHALL fall back to case-insensitive exact string matching for that answer and record grading_method as "exact" with a confidence_score of 1.0 or 0.0.
9. THE Question_Generator SHALL persist all grading results as immutable QuizAttempt records consistent with the existing audit trail requirements.

### Requirement 6: Role-Play Scenarios — Virtual Audit Engine

**User Story:** As a training coordinator, I want to offer employees an interactive "Virtual Audit" experience where they must demonstrate document comprehension through a conversational dialogue, so that training assessment goes beyond static quizzes to test practical application.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Virtual Audit session is initiated for a user and document, THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL create a Virtual_Audit_Session record and begin an interactive conversation using the Educational_Specialist agent archetype configured with temperature 0.4 (lower than default for consistent evaluation).
2. WHEN a Virtual_Audit_Session is created, THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL construct a system prompt that instructs the LLM to act as an auditor conducting a compliance audit, asking the employee questions about the document content in a progressive difficulty sequence: foundational questions for the first 40% of turns, applied questions for the next 35% of turns, and analytical questions for the remaining 25% of turns.
3. THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL determine the total number of conversational turns per session based on document length: 5 turns for documents with fewer than 10 sections, 7 turns for documents with 10–20 sections, and 10 turns for documents with more than 20 sections. Each turn consists of: an auditor question, the employee's response, and an immediate evaluation of the response quality.
4. THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL evaluate each employee response on three dimensions: factual_accuracy (0.0–1.0, does the response contain correct information from the document), completeness (0.0–1.0, does the response address all aspects of the question), and document_reference_quality (0.0–1.0, does the response demonstrate knowledge of where information resides in the document).
5. THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL compute an overall session score as the weighted average of all completed turn scores: factual_accuracy (50%), completeness (30%), document_reference_quality (20%). Turns not completed (due to early termination) SHALL be excluded from the average calculation.
6. THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL apply a passing threshold of 0.70 overall session score for the Virtual Audit to count as "Passed". IF the user completes fewer than 3 turns before ending the session, THEN THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL mark the session as "Incomplete" rather than "Passed" or "Failed" regardless of score.
7. WHEN a Virtual Audit session is completed (all turns answered or user ends early after at least 3 turns), THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL produce a session_summary containing: overall_score, per_turn_scores, strengths_identified, areas_for_improvement, and recommended_review_sections (document sections the user should revisit).
8. THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL expose a POST /api/training/roleplay/start endpoint that accepts {document_id, document_version_id, user_id} and returns the session_id, first auditor question, and total_turns. IF the user already has a Virtual_Audit_Session with status "in_progress" for the same document_version_id, THEN THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL return the existing session_id and current state instead of creating a new session.
9. THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL expose a POST /api/training/roleplay/{session_id}/respond endpoint that accepts {response_text} (maximum 2000 characters) and returns the evaluation of the current response plus the next auditor question (or session completion if all turns are done). IF response_text exceeds 2000 characters, THEN THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL reject the request with an error indicating the maximum length.
10. THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL expose a GET /api/training/roleplay/{session_id} endpoint that returns the full session state including all turns, scores, and summary (if completed).
11. THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL persist all session data (questions, responses, evaluations) as immutable records for audit trail compliance. IF persistence fails during a turn evaluation, THEN THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL return an error to the user and retain the session in its previous consistent state without advancing the turn counter.
12. THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL scope all operations to the company identified by the X-Company-Id header, and POST endpoints SHALL require the X-Change-Reason header.
13. IF a Virtual_Audit_Session with status "in_progress" receives no response for 60 minutes, THEN THE Role_Play_Engine SHALL mark the session status as "abandoned" and compute the score based on completed turns only. An abandoned session with fewer than 3 completed turns SHALL be marked "Incomplete".

### Requirement 7: Dynamic Feedback — Paragraph-Level Guidance

**User Story:** As an employee taking a training quiz, I want the system to point me to the exact paragraph in the source document where the correct answer resides when I answer incorrectly, so that I can learn from my mistakes efficiently without searching through the entire document.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user answers a question incorrectly, THE Dynamic_Feedback_Service SHALL retrieve the Paragraph_Reference for the correct answer by querying the RAG knowledge base (4.2) with the question text and correct answer as the search query, scoped to the specific source document.
2. THE Dynamic_Feedback_Service SHALL return a feedback response containing: the correct_answer, the paragraph_text (exact text from the source document containing the answer), the section_reference (section title and paragraph number), a page_number (if available from document metadata), and an explanation (LLM-generated explanation of why this paragraph answers the question).
3. THE Dynamic_Feedback_Service SHALL use the embedding model to compute semantic similarity between the correct_answer and candidate paragraphs, selecting the paragraph with the highest similarity score above a threshold of 0.75.
4. IF no paragraph achieves a similarity score above 0.75, THEN THE Dynamic_Feedback_Service SHALL fall back to returning the sop_section_ref stored with the question and a generic message indicating the section to review.
5. THE Dynamic_Feedback_Service SHALL generate the explanation using the InferenceClient with a prompt that includes the question, correct answer, and source paragraph, instructing the LLM to explain the connection in 2-3 sentences.
6. THE Dynamic_Feedback_Service SHALL cache paragraph references for previously answered questions (keyed by question_id + document_version_id) to avoid redundant RAG queries on repeated attempts. WHEN a new document version is published, THE cache entries for the previous version SHALL be invalidated.
7. THE Dynamic_Feedback_Service SHALL expose a GET /api/training/feedback/{question_id} endpoint that returns the dynamic feedback for a specific question, including paragraph reference and explanation. This endpoint SHALL only return feedback if the requesting user has at least one failed attempt for this question, returning HTTP 403 otherwise.
8. THE Dynamic_Feedback_Service SHALL scope all operations to the company identified by the X-Company-Id header.
9. IF the InferenceClient is unavailable or fails to respond within 30 seconds during explanation generation, THEN THE Dynamic_Feedback_Service SHALL return the paragraph_text and section_reference without the LLM-generated explanation, substituting a generic message: "Review the highlighted paragraph for the correct information."
10. THE Dynamic_Feedback_Service SHALL respond to GET /api/training/feedback/{question_id} within 5 seconds when a cached entry exists, and within 30 seconds for uncached queries requiring RAG retrieval and LLM explanation generation.

### Requirement 8: Training Ecosystem Database Models

**User Story:** As a backend developer, I want well-defined database models for training schedules, generated materials, assessment questions, role-play sessions, and feedback records, so that all AI-generated training data is persisted with full audit trail support.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE TrainingSchedule model SHALL include: id, user_id (FK to users), company_id (FK to companies), schedule_data (JSONB containing ordered training items with priorities, deadlines, and dependencies), compliance_percentage (float 0.0–100.0), total_items (integer >= 0), completed_items (integer >= 0), generated_at (timestamp), last_recalculated_at (timestamp), created_at, updated_at. THE model SHALL enforce a check constraint that completed_items <= total_items, and a unique constraint on (user_id, company_id) ensuring one active schedule per user per company.
2. THE SkillGap model SHALL include: id, user_id (FK to users), company_id (FK to companies), document_id (FK to documents), document_version_id (FK to document_versions), gap_type (enum: missing_training, expired_training, new_version_available), priority (enum: Critical, High, Medium, Low), days_overdue (integer >= 0, nullable), blocks_access (boolean), identified_at (timestamp), resolved_at (timestamp, nullable), created_at. THE model SHALL enforce a unique constraint on (user_id, document_id, document_version_id, gap_type) to prevent duplicate gap records for the same deficiency.
3. THE TrainingMaterial model SHALL include: id, document_id (FK to documents), document_version_id (FK to document_versions), company_id (FK to companies), material_type (enum: executive_summary, detailed_walkthrough, key_takeaways, presentation_outline, safety_highlights), content_data (JSONB containing the structured material), learning_objectives (JSON array), estimated_duration_minutes (integer, range 1–480), status (ContentStatus enum), generated_by_agent_id (String 255, nullable), inference_duration_ms (integer, nullable), reviewed_by (FK to users, nullable), reviewed_at (timestamp, nullable), created_at, updated_at. THE model SHALL enforce a unique constraint on (document_id, document_version_id, material_type) to prevent duplicate materials of the same type for a document version.
4. THE GeneratedQuestion model SHALL include: id, document_id (FK to documents), document_version_id (FK to document_versions), company_id (FK to companies), question_text (Text), question_type (enum: multiple_choice, true_false, scenario_based, fill_in_blank), correct_answer (Text), distractors (JSON array, nullable), explanation (Text), difficulty_level (enum: basic, intermediate, advanced), bloom_taxonomy_level (String 50), sop_section_ref (String 500), status (ContentStatus enum), reviewed_by (FK to users, nullable), reviewed_at (timestamp, nullable), created_at, updated_at.
5. THE VirtualAuditSession model SHALL include: id, user_id (FK to users), document_id (FK to documents), document_version_id (FK to document_versions), company_id (FK to companies), status (enum: in_progress, completed, abandoned), overall_score (float 0.0–1.0, nullable), passed (boolean, nullable), turns_completed (integer >= 0, default 0), total_turns (integer, range 5–10), session_data (JSONB containing all turns with questions, responses, and evaluations), summary_data (JSONB, nullable), started_at (timestamp), completed_at (timestamp, nullable), created_at. THE model SHALL enforce a check constraint that turns_completed <= total_turns.
6. THE DynamicFeedbackCache model SHALL include: id, question_id (FK to GeneratedQuestion), document_version_id (FK to document_versions), paragraph_text (Text), section_reference (String 500), page_number (integer >= 1, nullable), similarity_score (float 0.0–1.0), explanation (Text), created_at. THE model SHALL enforce a unique constraint on (question_id, document_version_id) to ensure one cached feedback entry per question per document version.
7. ALL models SHALL use the AuditMixin for SQLAlchemy-Continuum versioning where applicable (TrainingSchedule, TrainingMaterial, GeneratedQuestion, VirtualAuditSession).
8. ALL models SHALL include appropriate indexes: user_id + company_id composite indexes on TrainingSchedule and SkillGap for schedule and gap queries, document_id + document_version_id composite indexes on TrainingMaterial, GeneratedQuestion, and DynamicFeedbackCache for material and question lookups, status indexes on TrainingMaterial, GeneratedQuestion, and VirtualAuditSession for filtering, and user_id + document_id composite index on VirtualAuditSession for session history queries.

### Requirement 9: Training Ecosystem API Endpoints

**User Story:** As a frontend developer, I want comprehensive REST endpoints for the AI training ecosystem, so that the training dashboard can present schedules, materials, assessments, role-play sessions, and feedback to users.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE system SHALL expose POST /api/training/materials/generate that accepts {document_id, document_version_id, material_types (optional)} and returns HTTP 202 with {job_id, status: "pending"}. The request SHALL require the X-Change-Reason header.
2. THE system SHALL expose GET /api/training/materials/{document_id} that returns all generated materials for a document, supporting query params: material_type, status, limit (default 20, maximum 100), offset (default 0).
3. THE system SHALL expose PATCH /api/training/materials/{material_id}/approve and PATCH /api/training/materials/{material_id}/reject that update material status and require X-Change-Reason header.
4. THE system SHALL expose POST /api/training/questions/generate that accepts {document_id, document_version_id, question_count (optional, integer 5–20, default 10), difficulty_distribution (optional)} and returns HTTP 202 with {job_id, status: "pending"}. The request SHALL require the X-Change-Reason header.
5. THE system SHALL expose GET /api/training/questions/{document_id} that returns generated questions, supporting query params: status, difficulty_level, question_type, limit (default 20, maximum 100), offset (default 0).
6. THE system SHALL expose PATCH /api/training/questions/{question_id}/approve and PATCH /api/training/questions/{question_id}/reject that update question status and require X-Change-Reason header.
7. THE system SHALL expose POST /api/training/roleplay/start that accepts {document_id, document_version_id, user_id} and returns {session_id, first_question, total_turns}. The request SHALL require the X-Change-Reason header.
8. THE system SHALL expose POST /api/training/roleplay/{session_id}/respond that accepts {response_text (string, maximum 2000 characters)} and returns {evaluation, next_question (or null if session complete), session_complete (boolean), current_score}. The request SHALL require the X-Change-Reason header.
9. THE system SHALL expose GET /api/training/roleplay/{session_id} that returns the session record including: session status, overall_score, passed flag, turns_completed, total_turns, session_data (all turns with questions, responses, and evaluations), and summary_data (if session is completed).
10. THE system SHALL expose GET /api/training/roleplay/history/{user_id} that returns a paginated list of Virtual Audit sessions for a user, supporting query params: document_id, status, passed, limit (default 20, maximum 100), offset (default 0).
11. THE system SHALL expose GET /api/training/feedback/{question_id} that returns dynamic feedback containing: correct_answer, paragraph_text, section_reference, page_number (if available), and explanation.
12. IF the requesting user has no failed attempts for the question, THEN THE system SHALL return HTTP 403 on GET /api/training/feedback/{question_id} with an error message indicating that feedback is only available after a failed attempt.
13. IF a POST /api/training/roleplay/{session_id}/respond request is received for a session with status "completed" or "abandoned", THEN THE system SHALL return HTTP 409 with an error message indicating the session is no longer active.
14. THE system SHALL return HTTP 404 for resources that do not exist or do not belong to the requesting company.
15. THE system SHALL scope all endpoints to the company identified by the X-Company-Id header and SHALL return HTTP 400 if the X-Company-Id header is missing.

### Requirement 10: Frontend Training Ecosystem Components

**User Story:** As an employee, I want a unified training dashboard that shows my personalized training schedule, available materials, assessments, and role-play options, so that I can efficiently complete all required training in one place.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE TrainingEcosystemPage SHALL be accessible at route /training/ecosystem and display: the user's current training schedule with a progress bar showing completed_items / total_items as a percentage, skill gap alerts, available training materials per document, and assessment options (quiz or virtual audit).
2. THE TrainingSchedulePanel SHALL display training items sorted by priority (Critical first) then by suggested deadline (earliest first) in a timeline view showing: document title (truncated to 120 characters with ellipsis), priority badge (color-coded: Critical=red, High=orange, Medium=yellow, Low=blue), suggested deadline, completion status (not_started, in_progress, completed), and a "Start Training" action button.
3. IF the user has one or more Critical or High priority skill gaps, THEN THE SkillGapAlert component SHALL display a dismissible banner at the top of the training page showing the count of gaps and a link to the detailed gap view at /training/ecosystem?view=gaps.
4. THE TrainingMaterialViewer component SHALL render only materials with status "approved" to users with the trainee role, and SHALL render materials in the appropriate format: executive_summary as a card with key points, detailed_walkthrough as a step-by-step accordion, presentation_outline as a slide-deck carousel, and safety_highlights as a warning panel with a distinct visual border and warning icon.
5. THE VirtualAuditInterface component SHALL provide a chat-style interface for role-play sessions showing: the auditor's questions in left-aligned bubbles, the user's responses in right-aligned bubbles, per-turn score indicators (green for score >= 0.70, yellow for score >= 0.40 and < 0.70, red for score < 0.40), and a progress bar showing turns completed vs total_turns.
6. WHEN a user answers a quiz question incorrectly, THE DynamicFeedbackPanel component SHALL display: the correct answer highlighted in green, the source paragraph with the relevant sentence highlighted, the section reference as a clickable link that navigates to the document viewer at the referenced section, and the LLM-generated explanation text.
7. THE TrainingEcosystemPage SHALL use polling (every 15 seconds) to update generation job statuses (material generation, question generation) without full page reload, displaying each job as one of: "pending", "in_progress", "completed", or "failed".
8. THE TrainingEcosystemPage SHALL provide a "Generate Materials" and "Generate Quiz" action on each document that requires training, visible only to users with coordinator or admin roles. IF a generation job is already pending or in_progress for that document and type, THEN THE system SHALL disable the corresponding action button and display the current job status.
9. WHILE the TrainingEcosystemPage is loading data from the backend, THE page SHALL display a skeleton loading state for each panel. IF no training items exist for the user, THEN THE TrainingSchedulePanel SHALL display an empty state message indicating no training is currently assigned.

### Requirement 11: Integration with Training-Gated Access Control

**User Story:** As a compliance officer, I want the AI-generated assessments (quizzes and virtual audits) to integrate with the existing training gate (3.5), so that users cannot access regulated documents until they pass AI-generated assessments.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Question_Generator produces approved questions for a document version, THE system SHALL register those questions as the active question set retrievable by the existing quiz submission flow (POST /api/training/quiz/submit) using the content_id format {sop_document_uuid}_v{sop_version}, replacing any previously active question set for that content_id.
2. THE system SHALL allow either a passed quiz attempt OR a passed Virtual Audit session to satisfy the comprehension verification requirement of the training gate (3.5), such that the existing has_user_passed check (GET /api/training/quiz/passed/{content_id}) returns has_passed=true when either condition is met.
3. WHEN a user passes a Virtual Audit session (overall_score >= 0.70), THE system SHALL create a QuizAttempt record with passed=true, content_id set to {sop_document_uuid}_v{sop_version} of the audited document, score set to the number of turns scored above 0.70, total_questions set to the total turns in the session, and answers set to an empty JSON object, to maintain compatibility with the existing training gate check.
4. THE system SHALL enforce that only approved questions (status=approved) are used in quiz submissions. Draft or pending_review questions SHALL NOT appear in active assessments.
5. IF a user attempts to submit a quiz for a document version that has no approved questions, THEN THE system SHALL return an error response indicating that no approved assessment is available for the requested document version.
6. WHEN a new document version is published and new questions are generated and approved, THE system SHALL mark previous question sets for the old version as inactive so they are no longer served to new quiz attempts, while preserving existing QuizAttempt records for audit trail purposes.
7. THE system SHALL maintain backward compatibility with the existing quiz_service.py by preserving the existing POST /api/training/quiz/submit, GET /api/training/quiz/passed/{content_id}, and GET /api/training/quiz/results/{content_id} endpoint contracts, the QuizAttempt model schema, and the 80% passing threshold logic, extending the question source to include AI-generated questions without altering existing response formats.
8. WHILE a document version has no approved questions and no user has passed a Virtual Audit for it, THE system SHALL report has_passed=false for all users on that document version via the training gate check endpoint.

### Requirement 12: Celery Task Definitions for Async AI Operations

**User Story:** As a backend developer, I want all long-running AI operations (schedule generation, material generation, question generation, grading) to execute as Celery async tasks, so that API endpoints return immediately and the system remains responsive.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE system SHALL define a Celery task `generate_training_schedule` that accepts (user_id, company_id) and, upon successful completion, produces a TrainingSchedule record persisted to the database.
2. THE system SHALL define a Celery task `generate_training_materials` that accepts (document_id, document_version_id, company_id, material_types) and, upon successful completion, produces one TrainingMaterial record per requested material type persisted to the database.
3. THE system SHALL define a Celery task `generate_questions` that accepts (document_id, document_version_id, company_id, question_count, difficulty_distribution) and, upon successful completion, produces between 5 and 20 GeneratedQuestion records persisted to the database.
4. THE system SHALL define a Celery task `evaluate_roleplay_response` that accepts (session_id, response_text) and, upon successful completion, updates the VirtualAuditSession record with the evaluation scores for the current turn and the next auditor question (or session completion data if all turns are done).
5. THE system SHALL define a Celery task `recalculate_skill_gaps` that accepts (company_id) and updates all SkillGap records for the company by comparing current training records against required documents. This task SHALL be registered in the Celery beat schedule to execute every 6 hours.
6. ALL Celery tasks SHALL implement retry logic with exponential backoff (max 3 retries, initial delay 30 seconds, jitter enabled) for transient failures including: inference timeouts, connection errors (ConnectionError, OSError), and model unavailability. IF all retry attempts are exhausted, THEN THE system SHALL mark the job as failed via the job_tracker service with an error message indicating the failure reason.
7. ALL Celery tasks SHALL record their execution status via the existing job_tracker service by: calling create_job at task start to set status to "processing", calling update_progress during execution to report percentage complete, and calling either complete_job on success or fail_job on failure with a descriptive error message.
8. ALL Celery tasks SHALL enforce a maximum execution timeout of 600 seconds (10 minutes) per task invocation using the Celery soft_time_limit setting. IF a task exceeds this timeout, THEN THE system SHALL catch the SoftTimeLimitExceeded exception and mark the job as failed via the job_tracker service with reason "timeout".
9. IF a task fails after producing partial records (e.g., some TrainingMaterial or GeneratedQuestion rows were persisted before the failure), THEN THE system SHALL leave the partial records in the database and record the failure in the job_tracker with an error message indicating partial completion and the count of records successfully produced.
10. ALL training ecosystem Celery tasks SHALL be routed to the "ai_operations" queue to isolate AI inference workloads from other background tasks (indexing, anomaly scanning).
